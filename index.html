Exhibiting Project

When presenting this project in the Central St Martins degree show. I wanted to foster an interaction with the audience to keep them engaged and feel apart of the outcome. I decided to wire up a custom button and have some simple code which displays the text frame when you press the button. This allowing people to decide the pace of the animation like a flip book. This really worked and was cool to see people press a button out of instinct and immediately being smashing it to see how quickly they can make the animation move!
